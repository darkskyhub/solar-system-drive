Five scenic routes

All roads lead to the Sun

Formally opened in 2008, the Drive spans more than 600 kilometres of bitumen across about 50,000 square kilometres of Central West and Northwest slopes country. Fuel, toilets and country hospitality wait in every town.

Route details

Queensland · Moree · Narrabri

From the North

Come down from Queensland through Moree and Narrabri. Pluto and Neptune sit at Bellata on the Newell Highway; Saturn is at Narrabri. All roads then meet in Coonabarabran for Jupiter on Timor Road.

Shared inner approach

Every route meets at Coonabarabran. From there, Timor Road carries Jupiter (about 7 km west, right-hand side), then Mars. Turn onto Observatory Road for Earth & Moon, Venus and Mercury, and finish at the AAT dome — the Sun of the entire model — at Siding Spring.

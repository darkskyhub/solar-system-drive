Earth & Moon

Shared inner approach · Timor Road

Nearly 71% of Earth’s surface is water. The Moon steadies our axial tilt and raises the tides. Without it, climate would be far more chaotic. The Earth–Moon pair slow Earth’s spin by about two milliseconds per century.

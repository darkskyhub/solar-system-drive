The red planet

Mars

Shared inner approach · Timor Road

Olympus Mons stands 22 km high — three times Everest. Valles Marineris would span the width of Australia. Two tiny moons, Phobos (Terror) and Deimos (Panic), are named for the horses of the Roman war-god’s chariot.

Country around the board

On the Warrumbungle approach the volcanic landscape echoes Mars itself. Kamilaroi people read this country for water, game and seasonal signs — skills any future Mars explorer would recognise.

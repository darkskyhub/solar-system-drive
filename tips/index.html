On the road

Make the drive the holiday

Slow down. Stop often. Connect with Country. Fuel, toilets and a welcome wait in every major town — and a handful of tables are worth planning the day around.

Tables worth stopping for

  • Robin Hood Inn

    Jerries Plains · Hunter / Merriwa route

    Classic country pub meals and a friendly midway stop.

  • The Vines, Hollydene Estate

    Near Denman · Hunter route

    Cellar door, excellent food, and fast EV chargers — the one to plan a longer break around.

  • Zin House, Lowe Wine Estate

    Mudgee

    Fine dining in vineyard country. Book ahead.

  • Silver Fox Café

    Gulgong

    Casual, historic gold-rush town. Perfect before or after the Pluto / Neptune / Uranus boards.

EV charging

Hollydene Estate (near Denman, Hunter route) has fast chargers and is the one to plan around. Other towns have standard chargers — check PlugShare for live status. The routes are increasingly EV-friendly.

Fuel & toilets

Available in all major towns. Most have 24-hour fuel. Fill in the larger centres for the best price.

Merriwa · Gulgong · Dunedoo · Coolah · Binnaway · Gilgandra · Gulargambone · Narrabri · Gunnedah · Coonabarabran

How to find a board

Search “Saturn Solar System Drive Gulargambone” or “Pluto Solar System Drive Merriwa”. The boards are large, dark blue, and unmistakable once you know the look.

  • Best light for billboard photos is early morning or late afternoon — golden hour on the 3D planets.
  • Bring binoculars. Some stops have a fine view of the real night sky as well as the model.
  • Download offline maps. Signal can fade in the Pilliga and the Warrumbungles.
  • Search “[Planet] Solar System Drive [Town]” in Google Maps — the boards are large, dark blue, and unmistakable.
  • Stay on paths. Take only photos. The old stories of this country ask for a light tread.
